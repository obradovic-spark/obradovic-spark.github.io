Metrics for this assessment demonstrate variability for children between 3 and 10 years old in Spring/Summer 2025 (N = 440, M = 4.5 years) and Fall 2025 (N = 679, M = 5.9 years). The Crafting task further demonstrates evidence of convergent validity with teacher-reported curiosity and creativity, as well as with child-reported creativity and challenge preference. It is also positively correlated with other curiosity and creativity tasks. The Crafting task metrics show a significant, positive linear association with age, indicating sensitivity to developmental change. The validation results do not differ based on child gender.
